# Glossary

- **Study spot**: A campus place a student can use to study, described by its building and, when useful, a particular floor or area.
- **Floor option**: A floor or area within a building that can have its own busyness and noise reports.
- **Community suggestion**: A study spot shared by a student and labeled as student-submitted.
- **Crowd report**: A student's report of a spot's busyness and noise at a particular time.
- **Crowd vote**: A student's 1–5 crowd rating for a spot or floor; only their latest rating contributes to the current result.
- **Live crowd status**: The median of at least three browser votes for a spot or floor within the last hour.
- **Busyness**: How full a spot feels, rated from 1 (empty) to 5 (packed).
- **Noise level**: How loud a spot feels, rated from 1 (quiet) to 5 (very loud).
- **Amenity**: A useful feature or nearby resource, such as outlets, computers, books, games, or food.
